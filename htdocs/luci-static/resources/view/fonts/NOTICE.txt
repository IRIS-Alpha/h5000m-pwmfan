MiSans subset
=============

Source: npm package misans@5.0.0 (https://www.npmjs.com/package/misans),
upstream MiSans by Xiaomi (https://hyperos.mi.com/font/).
License: Apache License 2.0.

Only the glyphs used by this plugin's user interface are kept: every
font chunk is subset with fontTools to the intersection of its
unicode-range and the characters present in the UI strings (34 woff2
files, 148 KB total, Regular and Demibold). Files are otherwise
unmodified; regenerate them with tools/subset-misans.py.
